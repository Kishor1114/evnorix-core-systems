# Evnorix Systems Design

You are an expert senior product designer, UX strategist, brand designer, and frontend engineer tasked with creating the complete public-facing website redesign for Evnorix.

I have attached:

The official Evnorix Website Redesign Challenge & Requirement Document

The official Evnorix company logo / branding asset

Treat the attached requirement document as the primary source of truth. Read and internalize the entire document before implementing anything.

The goal is NOT to create a generic IT-services website.

The goal is to create a premium, enterprise-grade, technically credible, conversion-focused website for a service-based technology company specializing in lending workflows, collections operations, and data infrastructure.

The final result should feel like a website that could genuinely be presented to a serious enterprise client and should look strong enough to impress the Evnorix hiring/evaluation team.

1. CORE BRAND POSITIONING

Position Evnorix as:

A service-based technology company that builds and delivers dependable digital systems and technology services that solve real business problems.

Evnorix should feel:

Professional

Mature

Technically capable

Reliable

Practical

Trustworthy

Outcome-focused

Enterprise-ready

The website should communicate business value first and technology capability second.

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT position Evnorix as:

A SaaS product company

A packaged software product company

A startup selling a single product

A generic software-development agency

A company with a large product portfolio

A company with invented enterprise clients or achievements

The three primary areas that must be communicated clearly are:

Loan Origination System (LOS) Services

Collections Services

Database Repositories & Data Mirroring

These three services should become the core visual and information architecture of the website.
